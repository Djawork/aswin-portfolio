01 / LITTLE ABOUT ME

ASWIN

ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN
3D VISUALIZATION
BUILDING PERMIT DOCUMENTATION

Aswin is a Professional 3D Visualizer and Architectural Draftsman specializing in architectural planning, building permit documentation and photorealistic architectural visualization. His professional approach combines technical expertise, creative exploration and an understanding of traditional planning principles to deliver architectural solutions that balance functionality, aesthetics and practical requirements.

With authorization to prepare building permit drawings within his applicable licensing scope, he undertakes architectural drafting and documentation for residential projects, translating design concepts into detailed technical drawings suitable for regulatory submissions.

An integral aspect of his planning approach is the incorporation of Vastu principles, thoughtfully integrating traditional spatial guidelines with contemporary architectural design requirements. This approach aims to create environments that respond to individual needs while maintaining spatial harmony, comfort and functionality.

02 / DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
“Great architecture begins with thoughtful planning and evolves through creativity, precision and an understanding of the spaces we inhabit.”

Aswin believes that every building should reflect a balance between its architectural identity, functional requirements and surrounding environment.

His design philosophy emphasizes intelligent space planning, natural lighting, ventilation, material selection and spatial relationships. By combining contemporary architectural concepts with Vastu-based planning principles, he aims to develop spaces that are aesthetically refined, functionally efficient and contextually appropriate.
