A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Design-minded. Detail-driven.

I’m Aswin Biju, an Architectural Drafting and 3D Designing practitioner based in Kerala, India. Since 2023, I have been focused on turning architectural concepts into clear, accurate working drawings and atmospheric visual presentations.

My work spans residential exterior perspectives, interior mood and lighting studies, technical floor plans, and Local Self Government (LSGD) permit & post-permit documentation—bridging creative spatial storytelling with buildable precision.
